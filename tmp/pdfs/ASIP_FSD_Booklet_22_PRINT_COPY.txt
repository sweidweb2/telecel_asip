Selection Day Mentor Feedback
Judging Criteria
The following criteria should be used when judging teams during 
each startup session. 
Team
Skills: Does the team have the necessary skills/experience or potential to succeed in their market?
Leadership: Are the Founders leaders? Can they inspire others to follow them?
Commitment: Are they passionate and hungry to grow?
Product
Solution: Is it solving a big problem or just a nice to have?
Scalability: Does it have traction thus far and in coming months, is the product scalable?
Market
Timing: Is it the right moment for the product?
Competitive Advantage: Is this a crowded market? Are the competitors not solving the problem well?
