FMO is the Dutch Entrepreneurial Development bank. 
As a leading impact investor, FMO supports sustainable private sector growth in

BD Waste
Every day, millions of tons of plastic waste are disposed of in landﬁlls and water bodies, 
threatening marine and human life. We believe that by using plastic as a means of 
saving, we can help people who do not have access to bank accounts—particularly those 
living in slum communities and low/middle-income areas—start saving money.
BD Waste is a GreenTech startup that combines ﬁntech and sustainability to recover 
Plastic waste from communities. The company's Digicycle product allows customers to 
deposit plastic waste into their digital wallets and receive credit for it, which can be 
used to purchase various items. BD Waste collects and processes over 50 tons of plastic 
waste per month, and has been recognized by the World Economic Forum and the Africa 
Development Bank for its efforts to address plastic pollution and use technology to solve 
societal issues.
Our B2B customers are mainly recycling plants in Europe who need high volumes of 
recyclable plastic waste for production. They use our service to access volumes of rPET 
bottles for production. 
B2C: Our B2C customers are mainly individuals who generate volumes of recyclable 
plastic waste. They use our service to access pickup services.

shipping APIs to provide delivery of cargo to their clients. We bring in the goods, deliver to the delivery 
company, and they proceed to handle the last mile delivery to Nigerian doorsteps. 
They use the service because they don't have to re-invent the wheel. They simply plug into our service 
as we have developed the expertise over 9 years before pivoting into this API solution.

Moja Ride
Taxis and minibus drivers and transportation companies lack access to credit to ﬁnance 
their vehicles. This creates poor quality of public transportation and pollution for our 
cities. Banks willing to ﬁnance these transportation service providers lack reliable data 
to make credit decisions and manage these loans. 
This is a $1.1 Billion opportunity for Cote d’Ivoire alone. 
We provide digital fare payment service to transportation professionals and companies 
which is used to assess their revenue in order to qualify them for a loan. We provide 
ticketing services for drivers ofﬂine and online booking services, so we gain a complete

and POS device. 
We provide lenders with data and repayment service as well as ﬂeet management 
which makes a car loan as simple as a smartphone loan where a borrower cannot use 
their asset if a payment is missed.
