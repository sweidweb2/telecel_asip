About DER/FJ and D-Hub
The Delegation for Youth and Women's Entrepreneurship (DER/FJ) is an initiative of the Head of State of Senegal 
providing a response to the problem of employment and aiming to promote the employment of youth and women 
in Senegal. Created by Decree 2017-2123 of 15 November 2017, the DER/FJ's mission is to accompany beneﬁciaries 
through ﬁnancial and/or technical support.  
The objective of DER/FJ is to contribute to the structuring of value chains in priority sectors of the national econo-
my in pursuit of its mission to contribute to the ﬁnancial and socio-economic inclusion of women and youth 
through entrepreneurship. The DER/FJ offers a ﬂexible and adaptable service including ﬁnancing, formalisation 
and training of targets; and is governed by the principles of transparency, speed and territorial equity.
For more information on DER/FJ, visit https://der.sn/ Freephone number: 800 00 40 41
About the D-Hub
The D-Hub is a space set up by the DER/FJ and made available to the Senegalese and international digital ecosys-
tem. This space is a reference framework that federates and engages entrepreneurship and innovation actors 
around projects, reﬂections and animation actions through incubation and acceleration programmes, but also 
co-working spaces and ecosystem animation. Through the D-Hub, the DER/FJ is positioning itself as a laboratory of 
ideas and best practices in the digital and innovation sector, for the beneﬁt of all sectors. For more information on 
the D-Hub, visit https://der.sn/

===== PAGE 9 =====
About FMO
FMO is the Dutch Entrepreneurial Development bank. 
As a leading impact investor, FMO supports sustainable private sector growth in 
developing countries and emerging markets by investing in ambitious projects and 
entrepreneurs. FMO believes that a strong private sector leads to economic and social 
development and has a close to 50-year proven track record of empowering people to 
employ their skills and improve their quality of life. 
FMO focuses on three sectors that have a high development impact: ﬁnancial institu-
tions, energy, and agribusiness, food & water. With a committed portfolio of EUR 12.5 
billion spanning over 85 countries, FMO is one of the larger bilateral private sector 
development banks globally. 
For more information: please visit www.fmo.nl.

About Startupbootcamp AfriTech
Startupbootcamp AfriTech was launched in 2017 as the ﬁrst multi-corporate-backed 
pan-African startup accelerator. We run world-class accelerator programs, working 
with some of the most disruptive startups on the continent. We provide access to our 
global network of corporate partners, investors, and mentors. The SBC AfriTech 
portfolio currently has 60 African startups spanning 13 countries and operating in 
sectors that include FinTech, InsureTech, Logistics, Mobility, AgriTech and Digital 
Health.
With our support, 95% of SBC AfriTech alumni are still operational 2+ years after the 
program, and they have an aggregate valuation of nearly $1 Billion while 22% have 
female cofounders.

BD Waste
BD Waste is a GreenTech startup focusing on recovering communities' waste. It 
combines ﬁntech and sustainability to provide a recycling and ﬁnancing system to it 
users. Customers can deposit their waste into a digital wallet and receive credit for it, 
which can be used to purchase various goods and services, including data packages, 
airtime, food, stationery, and health insurance.
Every day, millions of tons of plastic waste are disposed of in landﬁlls and water bodies, 
threatening marine and human life. We believe that by using plastic as a means of 
saving, we can help people who do not have access to bank accounts—particularly those 
living in slum communities and low/middle-income areas—start saving money.
BD Waste is a GreenTech startup that combines ﬁntech and sustainability to recover 
Plastic waste from communities. The company's Digicycle product allows customers to 
deposit plastic waste into their digital wallets and receive credit for it, which can be 
used to purchase various items. BD Waste collects and processes over 50 tons of plastic 
waste per month, and has been recognized by the World Economic Forum and the Africa 
Development Bank for its efforts to address plastic pollution and use technology to solve 
societal issues.

Cargoplug
Cargoplug, a spinoff of Jand2Gidi, brings its B2C expertise of almost a decade to
offer a simple shipping API that integrates into the checkout process and minimizes
uncertainties and transit times of shipping to and within Africa. The company
specializes in cross-border shipments, providing rapid transit times of as little as
24-48 hours from the UK to the African continent. Additionally, Cargoplug manages
customs and logistics procedures, making it easy for customers to ship goods from
around the world to Africa.
In addition, we provide intuitive dashboards and incorporate secure plug-ins
compatible with prominent international and domestic business platforms. This
seamless integration ensures complete visibility of deliveries throughout the supply
chain for businesses. Our comprehensive solution empowers local vendors to
expand their market presence while enabling global brands to penetrate new
markets.
Since the pilot, they have onboarded a number of customers including VC-funded 
ﬁntech, logitech and aggregators. They were also featured on CNN's Inside Africa in 
September 2022, as women leading the tech boom in West Africa.

Moja Ride
Moja Ride helps transport operators, taxi and minibus drivers track their earning through a 
powerful and simple ticketing and payment solution.We offer a drive to own program to the 
most performing unbanked drivers. We reduce the risk of default through a combination of 
data driven underwriting and a prepaid model which allowed us a zero default rate since we 
launched over a year ago. 
We have made it our mission to enable better and cleaner mobility services to African cities by 
making ﬁnancing easy and accessible for all transportation professionals. 
Drivers and transport companies can easily qualify for new car loans and car repairs by simply 
working with Moja Ride's advanced digital fare payment and booking technologies. We 
leverage the drivers' revenue and AI/machine learning to qualify them for car loans. Our 
solution is the ﬁrst alternative ﬁnancing solution for those who otherwise do not have access 
to ﬁnancing which requires no down payment or collateral.
