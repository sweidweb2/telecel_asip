About DER/FJ and D-Hub
The Delegation for Youth and Women's Entrepreneurship (DER/FJ) is an initiative of the Head of State 
of Senegal providing a response to the problem of employment and aiming to promote the employment 
of youth and women in Senegal. Created by Decree 2017-2123 of 15 November 2017, the DER/FJ's mission 
is to accompany beneﬁciaries through ﬁnancial and/or technical support.  
The objective of DER/FJ is to contribute to the structuring of value chains in priority sectors of the 
national economy in pursuit of its mission to contribute to the ﬁnancial and socio-economic inclusion 
of women and youth through entrepreneurship. The DER/FJ offers a ﬂexible and adaptable service 
including ﬁnancing, formalisation and training of targets; and is governed by the principles of transpar-
ency, speed and territorial equity.
For more information on DER/FJ, visit https://der.sn/ Freephone number: 800 00 40 41
About the D-Hub
The D-Hub is a space set up by the DER/FJ and made available to the Senegalese and international 
digital ecosystem. This space is a reference framework that federates and engages entrepreneurship 
and innovation actors around projects, reﬂections and animation actions through incubation and 
acceleration programmes, but also co-working spaces and ecosystem animation. Through the D-Hub, 
the DER/FJ is positioning itself as a laboratory of ideas and best practices in the digital and innovation 
sector, for the beneﬁt of all sectors. For more information on the D-Hub, visit https://der.sn/

About FMO
FMO is the Dutch Entrepreneurial Development bank. 
As a leading impact investor, FMO supports sustainable private sector growth in 
developing countries and emerging markets by investing in ambitious projects and 
entrepreneurs. FMO believes that a strong private sector leads to economic and social 
development and has a close to 50-year proven track record of empowering people to 
employ their skills and improve their quality of life. 
FMO focuses on three sectors that have a high development impact: ﬁnancial institu-
tions, energy, and agribusiness, food & water. With a committed portfolio of EUR 12.5 
billion spanning over 85 countries, FMO is one of the larger bilateral private sector 
development banks globally. 
For more information: please visit www.fmo.nl.

About Startupbootcamp AfriTech

10mg Pharma
10mg Pharma, a recipient of numerous awards, operates an AI-driven 
supply chain for chronic pain medications in Africa. By cutting out supply 
chain intermediaries and establishing direct partnerships with 
manufacturers through advance ﬁnancing, the startup achieves signiﬁcant 
cost efﬁciencies. 
This approach enables up to a 23% discount on standard medication costs, 
boosting proﬁt margins for healthcare providers by 3% and providing 
patients with savings of up to 15%. 
Using their innovative code tree model, 10mg Pharma is building a robust 
database infrastructure that eliminate ﬁnancial hurdles and facilitate 
cross-border low-interest lending without collaterals for healthcare 
providers in underserved African communities.

Edusko
Jide Ayegbusi  |  informjiday@yahoo.com  |  www.edusko.com/
Nigeria   |  Startup Stage  |   EdTech  |  2021  |  14 Employees
Over one billion children will be born over the next 3 decades in sub-Saha-
ran Africa. In the same vein, the rest of the world will depend on Africa for 
talents. Sadly, millions of African parents are unable to provide quality 
education for these future talents due to poor learning outcomes in the 
public schools while the alternative better education in the private schools 
are not affordable. 
Edusko matches African kids with the right k-12 private schools and 
ﬁnance their education affordably. The Nigerian startup has listed over 
8,000 k-12 top rated private schools in Nigeria, generated over $400,000 in 
revenue with over 70% margin and was named top 50 EdTech Startup in 
Africa by Holon IQ in 2022

Kori Tech
Nazib Ba  |  nazib.ba@kori-tech.com  |  https://kori-tech.com/
Senegal  |  Growth Stage  |  Fintech  |  2019  |  4 Employees
KoriPass is an innovative payment system coming out of Senegal. 
The ﬁntech’s unique value proposition lies in using NFC technology to speed 
up transactions, handle change issues and signiﬁcantly reduce waiting 
times in many cash heavy sectors like transportations and small retail. 
The product is built to scale as it does not require a smartphone or internet 
which is a barrier in most Francophone countries. 
With Koripass, payments are simple,fast and hassle-free! The company 
won the ﬁrst prize at the Salon des Startups 4.0 Afritech Startup competition 
in 2022 and was among the 8 ﬁnalists of the Ecobank Fintech Challenge 
2023.

Munakyalo Agrofresh
Lwasa Ronald  |  munakyaloagrofresh@gmail.com  |  www.munakyaloagrofresh.com/
Uganda  |  Startup Stage  |  AgriTech  |  2021  |  4 Employees
Munakyalo Agrofresh provides solar powered cold stations to smallholder 
farmers in Uganda. 
These extend the shelf life of perishable produce from the usual 2 days to 3 
weeks saving 80% food losses and giving farmers more time to search for 
buyers with better prices for their produce. 
The startup has managed to mobilize over 5000 farmers and has acquired 
over 3000 paying customers for their products. Munakyalo is also aggregat-
ing and offtaking the fresh fruits and vegetables which inturn enables the 
farmers to access markets and increase their incomes.

Messenger

Nigeria  |  Startup Stage  |   E-commerce | Logistics  |  2018  |  14 Employees

Primed E-Health
Dr Are Abdulhaﬁz  |  primeddiagnostics@gmail.com  |  www.primedehealth.com
Nigeria |  Growth Stage  |   Healthcare | FinTech  |  2016  |  27 Employees
Primed E-Health has been at the forefront of digitization the health space in 
Nigeria. They digitize small to large scale hospitals, clinics, and specialist 
practices in Africa using a simple, user-friendly hospital-based enterprise 
management system that increases efﬁciency in patient management and 
improves revenue generation by blocking ﬁscal leakages in hospitals. 
Primed has also launched “The Smartclinic” to address persistent challenges 
in healthcare in relation to the authenticity of patient data [to prevent health-
care fraud], paper-based record keeping [leading to pilfering and lost reve-
nue], cash-based transactions and payment difﬁculties [leading to long 
queues in hospitals]. 
Primed E-health has successfully trained and onboarded over 10,000 doctors, 
and directly impacted the lives of over 2,000,000 patients in the 45 hospitals 
that they operate in.
