About the Program
Startupbootcamp AfriTech built the ﬁrst multi-corporate backed accelerator program in Africa, and are 
ranked as the #1 Accelerator on the continent. We are the leaders in Corporate Startup Collaboration and 
we connect startups with opportunities for pilots, proof of concept projects, and commercial engagements 
with Africa's most Innovative corporate partners. This provides disruptors with a clear route to market 
through strategic partnerships with leading banks, insurance companies, telecoms and retailers across 
the continent.
ASIP Accelerator Program Powered by Startupbootcamp AfriTech is a Pan-African accelerator program 
funded by the anchor partner, Telecel Group. The ﬁrst cohort is 100% digital from scouting to Demo Day, 
and our Accelerator Squared platform brings world-class instructors, mentors, and workshops into the 
palm of your hand to help create successful companies at scale. This 3-month virtual program will 
empower the 10 most disruptive startups in sectors including FinTech, InsureTech, AgriTech, eCom-
merce, Digital Health, and CleanTech
Each startup receives more than €500,000 in credits, perks, and beneﬁts, plus €15,000 in cash investment. 
In return, Startupbootcamp gets 8% equity in each company. We don't take board seats and we don't get 
preferred shares. We strive to be the world's most useful co-founders. 90% of the alumni from our ﬁrst 3 
cohorts are still operating and the average increase in valuation is more than 7x since Demo Day.
