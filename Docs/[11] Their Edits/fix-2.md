Hi Ali,

Following Moh’s latest review, I went through the current live version again.

The overall structure is now in a good place, so this is not a request to redesign the website. The next revision should focus on elevating the visual energy, founder experience and startup relevance while keeping the site credible and premium for investors, partners and institutional audiences.

Please address the below in the next version.

1. Overall creative direction

Keep the clean corporate foundation, but introduce more startup energy in selected areas, mainly:

• Homepage / landing hero
• Founder sections
• 2026 cohort section
• Impact stories and case studies
• Registration / next cohort calls to action

The site should feel closer to a modern startup, accelerator or innovation platform, not a traditional corporate CSR website.

Use the ASIP visual identity more confidently here. The approved ASIP multicolour palette can provide the additional energy, while Telecel remains clearly visible as the programme owner/endoser.

Please avoid making the entire website colourful. The contrast is important: clean and premium overall, more expressive where founders, innovation and the next cohort come to life.

2. Photography and imagery

Authentic photography should remain the priority.

Please use:

• Real ASIP founders
• Real programme/event photography
• Founder working environments
• Workshops, mentors, pitching and collaboration
• Real market/startup moments

Avoid:

• Generic AI-generated people
• Synthetic corporate imagery
• Generic robots, brains or circuit-board visuals
• Standard stock-office photography
• Repetitive Africa-map/network clichés

Where we do not have sufficient photography, use restrained graphic, colour, typography or motion treatments rather than artificial-looking people.

3. Homepage hero

The current headline, “African founders. Real solutions. Impact that scales.”, is strong and can remain.

However, the hero needs more visual energy and a stronger first impression.

Please explore:

• Stronger authentic founder image/video treatment
• More confident ASIP colours
• Controlled motion or animated graphic details
• Cleaner hierarchy between ASIP, Telecel and the founder story
• More visible next-step CTA

The opening should immediately feel like an African startup platform with international credibility.

4. Reduce “connect / connecting” language

Moh specifically asked us to reduce repeated use of “connect” and “connecting”.

The current build still uses this language frequently, including:

“Af﻿rican founders. Connected to opportunity.”
“ASIP is a Telecel Group Program connecting…”
“Connect African innovation to opportunity.”
“Connect the right energies…”
“The right connection can change a company’s trajectory.”

Please conduct a full copy audit and reduce repetition.

Suggested alternatives depending on context:

• Back
• Build
• Open access
• Accelerate
• Enable
• Bring together
• Move forward
• Scale
• Create opportunity
• Turn ideas into action

We should retain Connecting Energies only where it deliberately links ASIP back to the Telecel masterbrand, rather than repeating the language throughout the site.

For example:

Current: “ASIP is a Telecel Group Program connecting ambitious African technology founders…”

Suggested: “ASIP is a Telecel Group Program backing ambitious African technology founders with expertise, capital pathways, market access and relationships that help businesses grow.”

Current: “The right connection can change a company’s trajectory.”

Suggested: “The right opportunity can change a company’s trajectory.”

5. 2026 cohort direction

The current 2026 section is still broad:

“The next chapter starts with the builders.”

We now need to introduce the agreed strategic direction around applying AI to real businesses.

Working territory:

AI Advantage in Action

This should not turn the entire ASIP website into an AI website.

The AI direction should primarily sit within the 2026 cohort/next chapter area and explain the practical idea clearly: startups applying AI to existing business problems and creating measurable advantage.

Please prepare the section so the final theme can be inserted once locked.

6. Founder experience

The Founders page is strategically important and should feel more alive.

Please elevate:

• Founder photography
• Country / market visibility
• Sector identification
• Short proof point or outcome
• Clear profile interaction
• Stronger visual distinction between cohorts

The objective is for visitors to see real companies, real founders and real outcomes, rather than simply browse a database.

The current search/filter functionality should remain.

7. Impact page

The direction around verified impact is strong and should stay.

The current principle, “No number goes live without a paper trail,” is particularly useful and reinforces credibility.

Please make the impact section more visual through:

• Large proof numbers
• Founder/company attribution
• Short outcome stories
• Relevant founder photography
• Selected animated counters where appropriate

Please ensure every live number continues to have validated source, period, owner and permission before publication.

8. Partners and ecosystem

The partner area should feel credible but not logo-heavy.

Please keep the focus on what partners actually bring to founders: capital, technology, markets, expertise and commercial opportunity.

Also please reconfirm all logos currently displayed and ensure we have approval/permission for their use before final launch.

9. Motion and interaction

Introduce light, purposeful movement rather than heavy animation.

Recommended examples:

• Subtle headline reveal
• Founder image transitions
• Animated impact numbers
• Small hover states
• Progress/journey animation
• Light ASIP colour movement or graphic accents

Avoid excessive effects, page movement or animation that slows the experience.

Mobile performance remains critical.

10. Programme page QA

Please review the Founder Journey implementation carefully. The current build appears to contain the Discover / Select / Build / Connect / Commercialise / Grow sequence twice in the page structure.

Please confirm whether this is intentional responsive code or an actual duplication and clean it if required.

11. Data validation

Before final lock, please flag the following for confirmation rather than changing independently:

• Total number of ASIP companies/startups
• All impact statistics
• Cohort chronology and status
• Partner logos
• Founder/company information
• 2026 cohort theme wording

The current site shows 42 companies, so we need to confirm the final approved number before publication.

12. Final design principle

The balance we need is:

Corporate enough for investors and partners.
Energetic enough for founders.
Authentic enough to feel African and real.
Modern enough to represent where ASIP is going next.

Please apply these refinements to the current structure and share the revised live version together with a short summary of the changes made.

Thanks,
