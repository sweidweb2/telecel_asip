01 
Final Design Mandate 
What the revised experience must achieve before sign-off 
     
 
The website is primarily informative, but it must feel designed around what each audience is trying to do. 
The final pass is therefore not a visual decoration exercise. It is an experience-hierarchy correction. 
The six changes that matter 
• Create two unmistakable entry pathways from the landing page: Founder and Investor. 
• Elevate the 2026 AI Advantage / AI Next Cohort content into the first meaningful scroll, not lower in the page. 
• Reduce text density sharply. Replace long explanatory copy with hierarchy, proof, visual storytelling and 
progressive disclosure. 
• Move away from recurring corporate conventions: repeated red blocks, identical card grids, frames, safe 
symmetrical layouts and one visual rhythm on every section. 
• Use authentic founder and event photography as the human core, supported by contemporary data, AI and 
technology visual language. 
• Keep the experience credible and functional for investors and institutions while making it desirable to younger 
founders and startup audiences. 
DESIGN TEST 
If a founder lands on the homepage for five seconds, they should know what ASIP is, 
what is happening now, and where to go next. If an investor lands on the same page, 
they should immediately see portfolio quality, current activity and a route to engage. 
 
Do not solve this by 
• Adding more red, more cards or more animation to the existing layout. 
• Turning ASIP into an AI-only brand. AI is the 2026 priority layer, not the entire historical identity. 
• Using generic AI people, robots, neon brains, circuit-board clichés or synthetic startup photography. 
• Rewriting the entire site architecture or introducing unnecessary new functionality. 
• Copying another accelerator, VC or startup platform literally.

04 
AI Advantage / AI Next Cohort 
Make the 2026 priority impossible to miss without making the whole site AI-themed 
     
 
Recommended placement 
Place the 2026 module directly after the hero, or integrate it as a prominent hero-adjacent feature. It 
should be visible before historical programme content, because it answers “what is happening now?” 
Recommended content hierarchy 
• Eyebrow: 2026 NEXT CHAPTER 
• Headline route: Building the AI Advantage 
• Supporting route: AI Advantage in Action 
• One sentence: Focus on founders applying AI to real business problems and creating practical advantage in 
African markets. 
• Founder CTA: Explore the AI Next Cohort / Register Interest. 
• Investor CTA: View 2026 Activity / Explore Portfolio Opportunities. 
Visual treatment 
• Use authentic founder or startup imagery as the hero layer. 
• Add restrained AI/data cues: live data points, interface fragments, responsive grids, data trails, model/output 
patterns, code or system signals where contextually relevant. 
• Use motion to express iteration, intelligence and progress, not sci-fi spectacle. 
• Make the module visually distinct from legacy/history sections through colour, scale and interaction. 
• Keep the treatment premium, fast-loading and mobile-first. 
AVOID 
Robots, glowing brains, holographic heads, synthetic founders, blue-neon “future tech” 
clichés, generic circuit boards, or anything that makes AI look decorative rather than 
useful.

05 
Visual System Recalibration 
Distinctive enough to feel startup-native, disciplined enough to remain credible 
     
 
Colour 
• Use the existing ASIP multicolour identity as the expressive palette. Let magenta, blue, green, yellow and red 
create rhythm across sections. 
• Telecel red should remain an endorsement/recognition anchor, not the default background colour for every 
module. 
• White, black and neutral grey should provide breathing room and premium contrast. 
• Colour should help separate audience journeys, content types and actions, not merely decorate cards. 
Typography 
• Century Gothic remains the core brand type system. Create disruption through scale, cropping, weight, 
spacing, kinetic type and editorial hierarchy rather than random font substitution. 
• Headlines should be short, confident and visually dominant. Avoid dense title + subtitle + paragraph stacks. 
• If a secondary display typeface is proposed, present it as a controlled option first; do not deploy it 
inconsistently across the site. 
Layout 
• Break the repeated image-left / text-right and equal-card-grid pattern. 
• Use asymmetric editorial compositions, oversized proof points, variable card scale, edge-to-edge imagery, 
controlled overlap, split screens and occasional horizontal movement where useful. 
• Create 2 to 3 signature layout moments on the homepage rather than making every module unusual. 
• Preserve generous spacing. Disruptive does not mean crowded. 
Photography 
• Prioritise real founders, teams, events, demos, workshops, products and market context. 
• Use tighter, more human crops and moments of action. Avoid staged handshake imagery and generic 
business stock. 
• Where photography is limited, use brand-led graphic/data treatments rather than synthetic human imagery.

Page-by-Page Application 
Where the final design system should change the current experience 
     
 
HOME Dual audience hero; AI Advantage high; proof strip; founder/investor modules; founder stories; 
footprint map; partner value; live/current content. 
ABOUT Compress history. Turn 2019-2026 into a build-log style progression with strong years, short 
milestones and visual proof. Avoid corporate chronology paragraphs. 
PROGRAMME Present the journey as momentum: Discover > Build > Test > Accelerate > Scale. Use progressive 
reveal, not process boxes connected by lines. 
FOUNDERS Make founders the visual heroes. Bold portraits, company identity, country/sector/cohort tags, 
one-line “what they are building”, high-quality filtering and full-card interaction. 
IMPACT Use large verified numbers, founder outcomes, data visualisation and case-study proof. Every 
metric must have reporting context. Avoid isolated “vanity number” tiles. 
INVESTOR / ECOSYSTEM Show portfolio quality, sectors, geography, current/upcoming activity and how to engage. Keep 
participation routes visible. This can sit within Partners if no new top-level page is required. 
PARTNERS Explain contribution before logos: Technology / Capital / Markets / Mentorship / Commercial 
Access. Logo wall becomes supporting evidence, not the page concept. 
INSIGHTS Only show populated categories. Prioritise founder stories, current activity and portfolio news. 
Remove empty shells and internal implementation language. 
REGISTER / APPLY Keep simple, lightweight and status-led. No unnecessary visual complexity. Current application 
state must be unambiguous. 
FAQ / CONTACT Clean utility pages. No special creative treatment required beyond consistent premium micro-
interactions and strong mobile usability.

07 
Interaction & Motion System 
Primarily informative, selectively interactive 
     
 
Use interaction where it improves understanding 
• Founder / Investor route selection: immediate scroll, anchor or route transition. 
• Africa map: hover/tap nodes with verified founder/company/market information. 
• Portfolio: responsive filtering by sector, country and cohort with smooth state changes. 
• Impact: restrained count-up or reveal animations tied to verified metrics. 
• Timeline / programme journey: scroll progression with active state, not passive lines and boxes. 
• Founder cards: image, tag or statement transition on hover, with full card clickable. 
• AI Advantage module: subtle data/system movement that responds to scroll or pointer movement. 
Motion standard 
• Fast, purposeful and low-friction. Motion should clarify hierarchy or create momentum. 
• Prefer 200-500ms micro-interactions and lightweight CSS/SVG/GSAP-style motion over heavy video effects 
where possible. 
• No autoplay audio, no motion that blocks reading, no long intro sequences and no unnecessary parallax on 
mobile. 
• Respect reduced-motion accessibility settings. 
• Protect Core Web Vitals. Compress imagery, lazy-load noncritical media and keep the hero performant on 
mobile networks. 
PRINCIPLE The website should feel alive because the content reacts intelligently, not because 
everything moves.

08 
Primary Benchmark 
One functional reference, used for principles rather than imitation  
     
 
Antler, Where Founders Go Further, Faster 
Reference: https://www.antler.co/ 
Why this is the strongest single benchmark for this phase: it demonstrates how a global startup platform 
can serve founders and investors on the same homepage without becoming dense, corporate or 
confusing. 
What to learn from it 
• Founder and investor pathways are made visible very early in the experience. 
• AI-related founder content is treated as a current, high-value proposition rather than buried in programme 
history. 
• Copy is short, decisive and benefit-led. 
• Portfolio proof and founder stories do much of the persuasion instead of long institutional copy. 
• Large typography, strong photography and confident spacing create energy without visual noise. 
• Proof numbers are used as signals, not as entire content sections. 
What NOT to copy 
• Antler’s brand palette, typography, layouts, specific headlines, motion signatures or exact information 
architecture. 
• Its VC-first positioning. ASIP remains a founder platform backed by Telecel with its own African legacy and 
programme history. 
• Its global visual identity. ASIP should feel rooted in African founders, real markets and the existing ASIP identity. 
BENCHMARK TEST Use Antler to challenge the current level of clarity and confidence. The final ASIP 
design should be recognisably ASIP, not “Antler in Telecel colours.”

Final Acceptance Criteria 
The website is ready only when the experience passes these checks 
     
 
AUDIENCE Founder and Investor pathways are visible above the fold on desktop and within the first 
mobile screen. 
CURRENT RELEVANCE AI Advantage / AI Next Cohort appears within the first meaningful scroll and clearly signals 
2026. 
COPY DENSITY No core homepage module depends on a long paragraph. Content is broken into 
hierarchy, proof, cards, tags or progressive disclosure. 
VISUAL DISTINCTIVENESS The homepage is not built from repeated red sections, identical card grids or conventional 
corporate split layouts. 
FOUNDER-FIRST Real founders, teams, events and startup outcomes carry the story. No generic/synthetic 
founder imagery. 
INVESTOR CREDIBILITY Portfolio quality, verified proof, sectors, geography, partners and current/upcoming activity 
are easy to find. 
AI TREATMENT AI is expressed through practical use, data/system behaviour and real businesses, not 
clichés. 
BRAND ASIP multicolour identity leads. Telecel endorsement remains visible and disciplined without 
dominating every screen. 
LANGUAGE Repeated use of “connect / connected / connecting / connection” is reduced outside 
approved brand language. 
MOTION Motion is purposeful, accessible and performance-safe. Reduced-motion fallback is 
supported. 
MOBILE All core interactions, CTAs, filters, map and content hierarchy are intentionally designed for 
mobile, not merely stacked. 
QUALITY No placeholder copy, empty categories, broken links, internal notes, duplicated FAQs or 
unverified claims remain at launch. 
FACTUAL DISCIPLINE 
AI-assisted concepts and final website content use verified ASIP data only. No invented 
startups, metrics, valuations, partner claims, cohort dates or investment opportunities 
appear anywhere in the experience.
